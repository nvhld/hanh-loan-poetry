# Translator Vocabulary Guard

Date: `2026-05-13`
Scope: Instrument Modal translator

The translator is a linguistic engine for observational fragments. It must sound like a small instrument reporting structural traces, not like criticism, prose poetry, or atmospheric narration.

## Hard Word Limits

- `FIELD MOTION`: maximum `11` words per line.
- `STRUCTURAL WEATHER`: maximum `14` words per line.
- `ABSENCE`: maximum `12` words per line.

Lines that exceed the section limit must be compressed before rendering.

## Banned Phrases

- `symbolizes`
- `represents`
- `means`
- `the poem`
- `the speaker feels`
- `emotional collapse`
- `proves`
- `as if`
- `while`
- `maintaining`
- `drifting toward`
- `continues`
- `remembers`

## Banned Cadence Patterns

- balanced explanatory clauses
- cinematic pause phrasing
- sentence pairs that sound like prose rhythm
- any line built around metaphor instead of measurement
- any claim about author, speaker, intention, or meaning

## Approved Vocabulary

- `detected`
- `elevated`
- `compressed`
- `reduced`
- `drift`
- `density`
- `field`
- `terminal`
- `aperture`
- `fragment`
- `signal`
- `residue`
- `motion`
- `thermal`
- `sequence`

## Max Clause Rule

One clause per line.

Accept:

`terminal density convergence.`

Reject:

`density converges toward terminal zone while motion continues.`

## Humility Constraints

- Observe, do not interpret.
- Prefer noun phrases and short detected states.
- It is acceptable for a section to feel incomplete.
- Cold gaps are allowed.
- Do not make the machine eager to explain.
